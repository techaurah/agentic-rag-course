# Solution for Lesson 28: Capstone spec

The most at risk gate on a larger, real questioned corpus is fact recall at 5: author written questions share vocabulary with documents, real questions bring the Lesson 07 gap at scale, and a tenfold corpus deepens candidate competition so gold chunks fall below 5 more often; the defence is the retrieval stack's measured levers (rewriting from real failure logs, decomposition for multi-hop, widening on grade failure) applied before any gate discussion. The gate to tighten for customer facing deployment is refusal: at threshold 2 the system answers one unanswerable question in six, and moving toward threshold 1 buys refusal 6 of 6 at the measured price of 9 false refusals in 26, a trade most deployments should refuse in that raw form; the defensible tightening is refusal 6 of 6 with false refusals at most 1, achievable only by adding an intent or entailment layer (Lesson 20's exercise), which is the honest way to state it: the gate names the capability the system must grow, not a dial setting it must endure.
