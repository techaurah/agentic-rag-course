# Solution for Lesson 03: Chunking from first principles

Run the variants and trust your own output over this text; on the reference build the pattern is: the 45 word target matches or slightly beats 90 on fact recall at 5 (short documents have little to dilute) while paying more chunks per answer, 180 drifts down as chunks re-approach whole documents, and overlap at 90 changes nothing measurable, because Fernhill's paragraphs rarely split a fact across a boundary. The shippable answer for this corpus is the plain 90 word packing. On a 10,000 document corpus of long reports the evidence that would change the decision is a golden set whose questions target boundary-straddling facts: if overlap lifts their fact recall by more than its context cost, it earns its words. The meta lesson is the table itself: chunking choices are empirical, per corpus, and cheap to test once a golden set exists.
