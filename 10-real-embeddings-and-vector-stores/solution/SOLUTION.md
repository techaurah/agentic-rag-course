# Solution for Lesson 10: Real embeddings and vector stores

The paper version's expected pattern, which installed runs on the reference machine broadly confirm in direction: the real model gains most on paraphrase and gap questions whose bridges are general English (greenfly to aphids, receipt to proof of purchase are in its training distribution), gains least or nothing on corpus specific naming (flagship to Fernbrook is Fernhill's private vocabulary; a general model cannot know it, which is why Lesson 15's rewriting table keeps a row no model can replace), and can lose on exact figure questions where dense vectors blur precise numbers and the lexical legs stay sharper. The professional summary is not "real embeddings win"; it is that each embedder has a failure profile, the golden and gap sets reveal it, and the hybrid design of Lesson 11 exists because no single leg's profile is acceptable alone.
