# Exercise for Lesson 09: The hashing embedder

Probe the stand-in's one trick and its limit. First, embed "guarantee" and "guaranteed" and print their cosine: the shared trigrams should place them close. Second, embed "greenfly" and "aphids" (the Lesson 07 gap pair) and print their cosine, then "refund" and "exchange". Rank the three pairs and write the conclusion the numbers force: which kind of similarity the stand-in captures, and which kind it structurally cannot. Finally, state which later lesson fixes the greenfly pair and by what mechanism.
