# Solution for Lesson 09: The hashing embedder

On the reference build the ordering is: guarantee pair highest (heavy trigram overlap), refund and exchange low, greenfly and aphids near zero (the words share almost no character trigrams, and no amount of hashing invents the entomology). The conclusion: feature hashing captures orthographic similarity, words that look alike, which covers plurals, tenses, and typos, and it captures nothing else. Synonymy is world knowledge, not shape. Lesson 15 fixes greenfly to aphids with a curated synonym table (query rewriting), and Lesson 10's real embeddings would also place them near each other because a trained model has seen them in similar contexts; the course keeps both answers visible so you can compare a knowledge based fix with a model based one.
