# Solution for Lesson 17: HyDE and expansion techniques

The gap questions' top scores separate imperfectly: the confidently wrong g5 retrieval scores in the same range as correct ones, which is Lesson 07's threshold lesson returning (scores do not certify relevance). A threshold set high enough to block g5's expansion typically also blocks one of the questions PRF leaves unchanged, and the rescued mean lands near 0.6667, the no-PRF baseline, rather than above it: the guardrail buys safety, not gains, because on this set PRF had no wins to protect (its before scores were already its best case). The design doc sentences: feedback expansion is allowed only when an independent grade (Lesson 24's checks, not the retrieval score) confirms the seed chunk contains the answer's facts; absent that grade, expansion is disabled, and the cost is the rare question where unguarded feedback would have helped, accepted in exchange for never amplifying a confident error.
