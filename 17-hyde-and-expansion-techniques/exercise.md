# Exercise for Lesson 17: HyDE and expansion techniques

Rescue PRF with one guardrail: apply PRF expansion only when the top chunk's BM25 score clears a threshold you choose from the data (print the top scores for the six gap questions first, then pick). Rerun and report the new gap mean and per question scores. Then write the two sentences that belong in a design doc: under what measurable condition is feedback expansion allowed in your system, and what does the guardrail cost the questions where PRF would have helped?
