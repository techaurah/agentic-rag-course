# Exercise for Lesson 27: The failure playbook

Take the five composition incomplete questions and determine, for each, which of two sub causes applies: (a) the facts live in different chunks, so no single sentence in the evidence contains them all, or (b) a single evidence sentence does contain them all but the composer ranked another sentence higher. Print the evidence for your verdict per question (the chunk and sentence that would have answered fully, if one exists). Then state what the (a) to (b) ratio tells a team choosing between a generating composer and better sentence selection.
