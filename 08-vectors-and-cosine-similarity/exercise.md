# Exercise for Lesson 08: Vectors and cosine similarity

Extend the toy set with a fourth chunk of your own that scores strictly between chunk 1 (0.4082) and chunk 0 (0.7071) against the question "cafe coffee", using raw counts only. Show your vector, your dot product, and your lengths. Then write a fifth chunk that a human would rank FIRST for the question but which scores 0.0 under counting, and state in one sentence what property a real embedding model would need in order to rank it first.
