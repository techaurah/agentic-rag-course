# Solution for Lesson 08: Vectors and cosine similarity

One valid fourth chunk: "coffee cake and tea" gives {coffee 1, cake 1, tea 1}; dot with the question is 1 (coffee), lengths sqrt(2) and sqrt(3), cosine 1 / 2.449 = 0.4082. That ties chunk 1 rather than sitting strictly between, a useful discovery: with tiny integer vectors the attainable cosines are a coarse ladder, and strict betweenness needs a longer shared-plus-unshared mix, for example "cafe serves coffee to gardeners and visitors daily" gives {cafe, serves, coffee, gardeners, visitors, daily} (6 terms): dot 2, lengths sqrt(2) and sqrt(6), cosine 2 / 3.464 = 0.5774, strictly between. A human first but zero under counting: "the espresso bar pours a great flat white" (about coffee, sharing no content token with "cafe coffee"). The property needed is a learned mapping where texts land near each other by meaning rather than by shared tokens: distributional semantics, which is precisely what Lesson 10's real embeddings add and Lesson 09's stand-in only gestures at.
