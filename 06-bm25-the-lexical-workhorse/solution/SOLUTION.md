# Solution for Lesson 06: BM25

On the plant care subset the sweep stays essentially flat: five similar documents with similar lengths give length normalisation nothing to separate and saturation nothing to punish. The knobs earn their keep when chunk lengths vary by orders of magnitude (transcripts beside FAQ entries) or when documents repeat terms aggressively (keyword stuffed pages, boilerplate headers in every chunk), because those are the pathologies b and k1 respectively exist to correct. The production measurement is the same sweep, run on the production golden set at production scale: vary one knob at a time, read fact recall and MRR, and only ship a change that moves a metric by more than the run to run noise you measured first. Anything else is numerology with better branding.
