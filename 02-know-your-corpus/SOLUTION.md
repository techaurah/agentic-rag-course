# Solution for Lesson 02: Know your corpus

A correct addition keeps the audit green: documents 25, all completeness checks True, and the new document appearing in exactly the role counts its manifest entry claims. The competition analysis is the graded part: a dog policy, for example, competes with nothing in the golden set (healthy isolation), while a new "delivery questions answered" page would compete with delivery and the FAQ for q02 and q15, and if its wording is vague it is a trap of the FAQ kind, whatever its author's intentions. The lesson generalises: in a real corpus, most new documents are written to be helpful summaries, and vague summaries are precisely what outrank precise policies. Governance (one canonical document per fact, summaries labelled as summaries) is a retrieval feature.
