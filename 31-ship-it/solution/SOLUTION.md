# Solution for Lesson 31: Ship it

There is no reference answer to compare against, which is the point of the exercise: your corpus, your numbers. A complete ship report has three properties the grader (you, in a week, having forgotten the details) can check. The gate table reruns from the harness, not from memory, and its values are whatever your corpus produced, including any red gates honestly reported. The known misses are classified by layer with question ids, and at least one miss should be composition or vocabulary shaped, because a report claiming no misses on a first corpus has not interrogated itself. The first upgrade follows from the table's largest class, not from fashion: retrieval misses argue for rewriting and decomposition work, composition misses for a generating composer under the checker, refusal misses for the intent layer. If your report has those three properties, you have not just finished a course; you are running the discipline the course exists to teach.
