# Solution for Lesson 24: Self-correction

The evidence grader triggers retries on more questions than the fact grader would (the composer fails or answers thinly on several questions whose facts are technically present), so the retry rate rises, typically into the 0.15 to 0.3 range depending on the pass bar, while q11 is now correctly caught whenever its composed top 3 answer lacks the facts, and final coverage at the widened depth reaches the Lesson 20 pipeline's level. The blind spot: the evidence grader can only verify that an answer is grounded in retrieved evidence, not that the retrieval found the RIGHT evidence, illustrated by q24, whose grounded, digit free, entirely wrong answer (a welcome sentence) passes every evidence check in the course. Grading answers against evidence is necessary; grading evidence against the world (the golden set, offline, forever) remains the outer loop no runtime grader replaces.
