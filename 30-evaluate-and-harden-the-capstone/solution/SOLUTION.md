# Solution for Lesson 30: Evaluate and harden the capstone

Run correctly, the permutation shows leaks at exactly zero for public and staff (the manager role legitimately sees everything, so its "leaks" are undefined), and the role sensitive questions are the three named ones: q19 and q32 answer only as manager, q20 refuses as public and answers as staff or manager (it asks for the staff figure, which staff may see). Any fourth role sensitive question is a finding: most commonly a paraphrase question whose gold document is public but whose best evidence sits in a restricted neighbour, or a refusal question that a higher role should answer. The incident report for the clean case is still worth writing, because a playbook entry that records a verified hold (class: none, evidence: 96 runs, owning layer: Lesson 12's pre-filter) is what lets a future maintainer trust the property instead of re-deriving it during a real incident.
