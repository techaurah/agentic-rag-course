# Exercise for Lesson 05: TF-IDF from scratch

Two experiments. First, remove the stopword filter from the tokenizer (in your copy of the artifact) and rerun `evaluate()`; record which of the three headline numbers moves most, and find one question whose top ranked chunk changes. Second, add the word "fernhill" to the stopword list and rerun; the change should be small but not zero. Write three sentences explaining both results in terms of IDF: what the filter was contributing, and why a brand name that appears in many chunks is nearly, but not quite, free to drop.
