# The Fernhill plant guarantee

## What is covered

Every hardy plant we sell is guaranteed for 12 months from the date of purchase. If a hardy plant dies within 12 months despite reasonable care, we will replace it or refund the purchase price.

## How to claim

Bring the plant, or clear photographs of it, together with your receipt, to any Fernhill store. You do not need the original packaging.

## What is not covered

The guarantee does not cover tender plants damaged by frost, plants that died from lack of watering during a holiday, or indoor houseplants. Bedding plants are guaranteed for 30 days only.
