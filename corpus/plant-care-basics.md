# Plant care basics for beginners

## Water and light

Most garden plants want a thorough soak now and then rather than a daily sprinkle. Put sun loving plants where they get at least six hours of direct sun.

## Feeding

A general liquid feed every two weeks in the growing season keeps most plants growing well. Follow the dose on the bottle.

## Watching for trouble

Check the undersides of leaves weekly. Sticky leaves, curled tips, and holes are early signs of pest trouble, and early action always works better than late action.

## Soil

Plants sulk in soil that stays waterlogged. Fork in grit or compost to open heavy soil up before planting.
