# Choosing compost and soil improvers

## Multipurpose compost

Multipurpose compost suits most pots, baskets, and general planting. It is the safe default for mixed containers.

## Ericaceous compost

Ericaceous compost is acidic, and it is the right choice for acid loving plants such as rhododendrons, azaleas, camellias, and blueberries. These plants yellow in ordinary compost because they cannot take up iron from alkaline soil.

## John Innes composts

John Innes composts are soil based and heavier. Number 2 suits potting on, and number 3 suits long term shrubs and trees in large pots.

## Soil improvers

Well rotted manure and garden compost improve heavy clay and hungry sandy soils. Dig them in during autumn or spring, before planting.
