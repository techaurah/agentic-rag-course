# The Fernhill seasonal gardening calendar

## Spring

Spring is for sowing seed, checking for early aphids on new growth, and feeding beds with compost as growth starts.

## Summer

Summer is for watering: pots in particular need attention in hot spells. Watch for slugs after rain, and deadhead flowers to keep them coming.

## Autumn

Autumn is for planting shrubs and trees while the soil is still warm, digging compost and manure into beds, and clearing leaves from ponds and paths.

## Winter

Winter is for pruning dormant shrubs, checking stored bulbs, and planning next year's beds by the fire.
