# Welcome to Fernhill

## Your new garden centre

Welcome to Fernhill Garden Centres. Whether you shop at Fernbrook, Millfield, or Coast Road, this pack explains the basics of shopping with us.

## Fern Club

Join the Fern Club at any till to earn points on every purchase. Points turn into vouchers, and our Gold members enjoy extra treats such as free delivery and free workshops.

## Deliveries and cafes

We can deliver locally for a small charge on smaller orders. When you visit, the cafes at Fernbrook and Coast Road serve lunch and cakes through the afternoon.
