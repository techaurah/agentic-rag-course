# Gift cards

## Buying a gift card

Fernhill gift cards can be bought in store for any value between 5 and 250 pounds. Gift cards cannot be bought with loyalty vouchers.

## Using a gift card

Gift cards can be spent in any Fernhill store, on plants, goods, and cafe purchases. A gift card cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Expiry

Gift cards expire 24 months after the date of purchase. Any balance left after the expiry date is lost.

## Checking the balance

Staff at any till can check a gift card balance. Balances cannot be checked by phone.
