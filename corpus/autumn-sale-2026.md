# Autumn sale terms 2026

## The offer

The autumn sale runs from 1 October 2026 to 31 October 2026. Shrubs and trees are reduced by 25 percent in all stores during the sale.

## Terms

Sale prices apply in store and on delivery orders. Staff discount cannot be used on sale items. Non-plant items bought in the sale can be exchanged but not refunded.

## Current terms

These are the current sale terms. They replace the spring sale terms for all new purchases.
