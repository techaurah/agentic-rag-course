# Working at Fernhill

## Current openings

We hire seasonal staff every spring for the plant areas and tills, and full time roles are advertised on the noticeboard in each store.

## Staff benefits

Staff benefits include a discount on plants and goods, free uniform, and training. The staff handbook gives the full benefit details.

## How to apply

Ask at any till for an application form, or write to the head office team through the contact page.
