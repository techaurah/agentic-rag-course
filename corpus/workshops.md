# Workshops and events

## The programme

Fernhill runs practical workshops every month at the Fernbrook store, on topics such as pruning, sowing seed, and planting containers.

## Cost and booking

Workshops cost 15 pounds per person and must be booked in advance at any till or by phone. Fern Club Gold members attend free, but still need to book a place.

## What to bring

Tools and materials are provided. Bring gardening gloves and wear clothes you do not mind getting dirty.
