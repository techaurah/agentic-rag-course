# Watering guide

## New plants

Water new plants deeply at planting time, then every two to three days for the first month while roots establish, unless rain has soaked the soil.

## Established shrubs and trees

Established shrubs and trees need watering only in dry spells lasting more than ten days. Water early in the morning or in the evening, at the base of the plant, not over the leaves.

## Pots and containers

Plants in pots dry out far faster than plants in the ground. In warm weather, check pots daily and water whenever the top of the compost feels dry.

## Lawns

A lawn needs about 25 millimetres of water per week in a dry summer. Watering little and often encourages shallow roots; water deeply once a week instead.
