# Fernhill cafes

## Which stores have a cafe

There are cafes at the Fernbrook and Coast Road stores. The Millfield store does not have a cafe.

## Cafe hours

Cafes open from 9:30 in the morning until 4:00 in the afternoon, on the days their store is open. The kitchen stops taking lunch orders at 3:30.

## Menu notes

The cafes serve sandwiches, salads, soup, and cakes. A full menu is posted in each cafe. Loyalty points are earned on cafe purchases, and gift cards can be spent in the cafes.
