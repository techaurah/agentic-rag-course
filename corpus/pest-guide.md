# Common garden pests and what to do

## Aphids

Aphids are tiny green or black insects that cluster on soft new growth and flower buds, especially on roses. Blast them off with a jet of water, or use the organic aphid spray sold in store.

## Slugs and snails

Slugs and snails feed at night and leave ragged holes in leaves; hostas and young lettuce are favourite targets. Copper tape around pots, beer traps, and wool pellets all reduce the damage.

## Vine weevil

Vine weevil grubs eat plant roots, and plants in pots suddenly wilt and collapse. Check the roots for white grubs if a potted plant fails without warning, and use the vine weevil treatment sold in store.

## Caterpillars

Caterpillars chew leaves on brassicas and fruit bushes. Pick them off by hand, or cover crops with fine mesh netting sold in store.
