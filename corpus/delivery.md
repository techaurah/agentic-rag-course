# Delivery policy

## Where we deliver

We deliver within a 15 mile radius of any Fernhill store. Orders are delivered Monday to Friday only. We do not deliver on weekends or public holidays.

## Charges and timescales

Standard delivery costs 6.95 pounds per order. Delivery is free on orders of 75 pounds or more. Orders placed before noon are usually delivered within 2 to 4 working days.

## Bulky items

Compost, gravel, paving, and other bulky items are delivered to the kerbside or driveway only. Drivers cannot carry bulky items through the house or into a back garden.

## Fern Club Gold members

Fern Club Gold members receive free delivery on every order, whatever the order value.
