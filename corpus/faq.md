# Frequently asked questions on the website

## Opening times

All Fernhill stores open at 9 in the morning. Closing times vary by store, so check the store hours page for the time your local store closes.

## Delivery

We deliver locally. Delivery usually takes a few working days, and larger orders may qualify for free delivery. The delivery page has the exact charges and timescales.

## Loyalty points

Fern Club members earn points on purchases and can exchange points for vouchers. See the loyalty page for the current rates.

## Gift cards

Gift cards last a good while, but they do expire eventually, so check the date when you buy one. The gift card page has the exact terms.
