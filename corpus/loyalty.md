# Fern Club loyalty programme

## Earning points

Fern Club members earn 1 point for every pound spent in store or on delivery orders. Points are added to your account on the day of purchase.

## Spending points

Every 100 points can be exchanged for a voucher worth 5 pounds. Vouchers can be used against any purchase except gift cards.

## Gold tier

Members who earn 500 points in a calendar year reach the Gold tier for the rest of that year and the whole of the next. Gold members receive free delivery on every order and free entry to Fernhill workshops.

## Expiry

Points expire 24 months after they are earned if they have not been exchanged for a voucher.
