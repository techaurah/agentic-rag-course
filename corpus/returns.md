# Returns and refunds

## Non-plant items

Tools, pots, gifts, and other non-plant items can be returned within 30 days of purchase, in their original condition, with proof of purchase.

## Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method. A refund usually reaches your account within 5 working days of the return being processed.

## Sale items

Non-plant items bought in a sale can be exchanged but not refunded. Plants bought in a sale are still covered by the plant guarantee.

## Faulty items

Faulty items can be returned at any time within 12 months for a repair, replacement, or refund, with proof of purchase.
