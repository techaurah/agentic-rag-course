# Contact and complaints

## Contacting us

Each store can be contacted by phone during opening hours, and the head office team answers email sent through the contact page on the Fernhill website.

## Complaints

We aim to acknowledge every complaint within 3 working days and to give a full response within 10 working days.

## Escalation

If a store cannot resolve a complaint, it is escalated to the head office team, who review the case and reply directly.
