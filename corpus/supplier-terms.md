# Supplier terms: Fernbrook Wholesale Plants

## Margin

Fernbrook Wholesale Plants supplies Fernhill at a trade margin of 38 percent below the recommended retail price on shrubs and 32 percent on perennials.

## Payment terms

Invoices are paid 30 days after delivery. A settlement discount of 2 percent applies to invoices paid within 10 days.

## Confidentiality

These terms are confidential to Fernhill managers. They must not be shared with customers, suppliers, or staff below manager grade.
