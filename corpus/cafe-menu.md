# Cafe menu

## Sandwiches and salads

The cafes serve sandwiches, salads, and a soup of the day. Sandwiches are made to order at the counter.

## Cakes and drinks

Cakes are baked for us by a local bakery. Teas, coffees, and cold drinks are served all day while the cafe is open.

## Prices

Prices are shown on the menu board in each cafe. The menu changes with the seasons.
