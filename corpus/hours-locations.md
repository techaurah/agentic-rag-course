# Store hours and locations

## Fernbrook (flagship store)

Our Fernbrook store is at 14 Mill Lane, Fernbrook. It opens Monday to Saturday from 9:00 in the morning until 6:00 in the evening, and on Sundays from 10:00 until 4:00.

## Millfield store

The Millfield store is at 3 Station Road, Millfield. It opens Monday to Saturday from 9:00 until 5:30 in the evening. Millfield is closed on Sundays.

## Coast Road store

The Coast Road store is at 220 Coast Road, Seabourne. It opens Monday to Saturday from 9:00 until 6:00, and on Sundays from 10:00 until 4:00.

## Holiday hours

All stores close on 25 and 26 December and on 1 January. On other public holidays, Sunday hours apply at every store.
