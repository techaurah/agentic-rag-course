# Solution for Lesson 07: Lexical failure modes

Good gap questions share a shape: the fact is unambiguous, the gold document is certain, and the surface words barely overlap. A model example: "My shrub has black sticky stuff under its leaves" (target: pest guide, aphids; shared words: leaves; missing bridges: sticky residue to aphid honeydew is beyond a synonym table, a reminder that some gaps need better documents, not better queries). Score your three honestly: a question that BM25 answers at 1.0 was not a gap question, whatever its wording felt like. The synonym pairs you name (receipt to proof of purchase, greenfly to aphids) become rows in Lesson 15's table, which is why the exercise asks for exactly one pair per question: rewriting works pair by pair, not by general cleverness.
