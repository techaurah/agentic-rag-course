# Solution for Lesson 18: Retrieval metrics that matter

The blends: TF-IDF (0.9808 + 0.9808 + 0.8731) / 3 = 0.9449; BM25 0.9529; vector 0.9679; hybrid 0.9616. The blend crowns the vector stand-in, hiding first that its worst question scores 0.0 (the hybrid's insurance property, Lesson 11's whole argument, vanishes), and second that TF-IDF and the hybrid tie on both recalls while differing hugely in MRR (0.8731 against 0.9231), a ranking quality difference the blend dilutes to noise. Under the blend, Lesson 11's fusion looks pointless (hybrid below vector) and Lesson 13's reranker verdict muddies (its fact at 1 gain and precision loss average out). The mandated format: the five metric table per retriever, per type breakdown for the shipped configuration, worst case named with its question id, and any single headline number required to travel with its table. A number that cannot be decomposed will eventually be used to deceive, usually by accident, always at the worst moment.
