# Solution for Lesson 26: Cost and latency budgets

Configuration A costs essentially compute you already own; its price is the quality ceiling Lesson 20 measured (21 of 26 full fact answers). For B and C the totals depend on the prices you pulled, but the structure is invariant: the generation call over the pack dominates, input tokens are set by Lesson 14's pack budget, and C's doubled retry rate adds its percentage almost linearly to the generation bill, which is why the grader's strictness (Lesson 24) is a financial control, not just a quality one. The single biggest lever is pack size: halving the average pack roughly halves the input token bill on every question, retries included, and Lesson 14's measurements (evidence concentrated in about five chunks) say the quality cost of a tighter budget is small and measurable. Spend on the composer (it lifts the 21 of 26 ceiling); save on pack words and retry discipline, and verify both savings on the golden set rather than assuming them.
