# Solution for Lesson 22: Routing and adaptive retrieval

A surface router typically routes singles and freshness correctly (they are short and quantity shaped) and stumbles on paraphrase questions that look simple ("Can I bring it back if I lost the receipt?" reads as one clause but needs the hybrid's resilience) and on q29, whose two questions hide in one sentence. Expect routed fact recall to dip on paraphrase or multi-hop by a few points against the label router's perfect table, with legs saved slightly higher (more questions look cheap than are cheap). The worst misroute is the confident one: a question the surface rules classify as simple with high certainty, where the uncertain default never engages. The fix is calibration, not more rules: any surface signal that has ever misrouted on the golden set stops being a certain signal, and the router's certain set shrinks to what measurement, not intuition, certifies.
